**IIT Bombay’s InstiApp** is a comprehensive, student-developed mobile app (by DevCom, IIT Bombay) that serves as a one-stop platform for almost every aspect of campus life—academics, hostels, co-curricular activities, events, recreation, and daily needs. It’s available on both Google Play and the App Store.

### Key Features of InstiApp (as of the latest updates)
- **Home page dashboard**: At-a-glance view with a **mess menu widget** (see what’s being served next without digging around) and **MyQR** (a unified QR code for campus services and access).
- **Profile barcode**: Scan your phone’s profile barcode at the main gate (handy if you forget your physical I-card).
- **Comprehensive Feed**: Real-time updates on everything happening on campus.
- **Explore section**: Discover all clubs, communities, and student bodies.
- **Blogs & Communities**: Includes Placement Blog, Internship Blog, Insti news collated from major bodies, and refreshed community experiences.
- **Institute Calendar**: Full list of events with details.
- **Buy & Sell portal**: Marketplace to buy/sell used items within the campus.
- **InstiMap**: Interactive campus map for navigation.
- **Quick Links**: One place for all important institute resources (academic portals, SSO login, webmail, calendars, etc.).
- **Emergency contacts**: Quick access when needed.
- Additional integrations: Hostel-related info, academics, co-curriculars, recreation, and notices.

It’s designed to reduce the need to switch between multiple websites or apps and is actively maintained with regular updates.

- Most have **official web portals** for academics (timetables, results, course registration — often called ASC, eAcademics, or similar), Gymkhana/student council sites, and hostel/mess info.

### General Pattern Across All IITs (Including Newer Ones)
- **Unified mobile apps** like InstiApp or InstiSpace are rare and usually student-led (DevCom, CFI WebOps, etc.).
- **Common backbone**: Web-based ERP/academic portals (e.g., for course registration, results, attendance tracking, fees). Many use institute-specific systems or platforms like Moodle.
- **Placements**: Almost always a separate portal/app (e.g., OCS).
- **Niche tools**: Complaints (Kanpur), attendance (Roorkee), events/Gymkhana (many), lost & found/buy-sell (Guwahati, Hyderabad), wellness/safety (Kharagpur’s SETU).
- **Newer IITs** (Patna, Tirupati, etc.): Heavily rely on web portals, Moodle, and basic apps; student initiatives occasionally fill gaps but nothing as mature as the ones above.
- No single “standard” across all 23 IITs — each develops or adopts what fits its needs. Official communication often still flows via email, institute websites, or Gymkhana portals.
